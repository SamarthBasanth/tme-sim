# Did the `tail90` recommendation survive its own falsification test?

**`results/p99_convention.md` recommends replacing the shipped `p99` bath-matching normaliser with `tail90` (CVaR-90), and names the experiment that would refute it. This file runs that experiment. The recommendation survives on the breast panel and is REFUTED on the GBM panel, for a reason the p99 audit itself identified and then did not price: `F_state`.**

Generated by `experiments/tail90_falsification.py`. Raw records:
`results/sweep/tail90_premise.json`, `tail90_ladder.json`, `tail90_dx12.json`,
`tail90_verdicts.json`.

> **This is a falsification test, and a negative result is a success.** No variant of `tail90` was tried in order to rescue it; the convention tested is exactly the one `p99_convention.md` §7.1 specifies (the mean exposure of the best-perfused 10 % of tumour cells). Nothing under `tmesim/` was touched, `BATH_REFERENCE_PCT` is unchanged, and no existing results file was edited. **No PDE transport solve was run.**

## 0. Bottom line

1. **The test's stated precondition is false. The tissue ladders' exposure fields do not exist on disk.** `experiments/grid_convergence_gbm.py` builds `e_unit_by_key` in memory (lines 477/560), returns it (line 615) and never writes it; the rung records carry only `mean / median / p01 / p99 / max` plus a sum-and-first fingerprint. `grid_convergence_breast.py` is the same. Enumerated across the whole repository, **25 per-cell exposure vectors exist, every one of them at `dx = 12 um`** (§1). A conditional tail mean is not recoverable from five order statistics, so `tail90` itself cannot be evaluated at any rung except the published one without re-solving. **The claim that this test "costs no new PDE solves" is wrong** — as specified it costs a full re-run of both ladders.

2. **A stronger test was run instead, and it is generous to the recommendation.** `tail90`'s grid error is not zero; a normaliser with **exactly** zero grid error is the limit of what any normaliser choice can buy. That counterfactual ladder is computable from the cached records alone (§2). **No real convention can beat it, so if the perfect-normaliser ladder is still badly behaved, `tail90` cannot fix it.** Over the fine rungs the substitution is nearly exact anyway: `tail90`'s own measured slab error is **≤ 0.17 %** at every slab rung with `dx ≤ 16 um` (`results/sweep/p99_gridslab.json`), against p99's +4.5 % to +9.5 %.

3. **GBM: the prediction is refuted, in every one of its three parts.** Predicted "closer to monotone, extractable order, smaller spread". Measured, over the fine rungs (`dx ≤ 18 um`), under a perfect normaliser:
   * **not closer to monotone — worse.** Direction reversals go from **3 of 5 steps to 4 of 5** for all three drugs, and the largest against-trend step grows from 3.07-5.15 % to **4.69-4.93 %**.
   * **not an extractable order — worse.** The power-law fit is DEGENERATE for **9 of 9** ladders under a perfect normaliser against **4 of 9** under p99, and the number of ladders yielding a physically plausible Richardson order (`0 < p ≤ 4`) falls from **3 of 9 to 0 of 9**.
   * **spread barely moves.** Fine-rung span **6.60/7.38/6.74 % → 5.79/5.49/5.53 %** for temozolomide/cisplatin/doxorubicin: a **12-26 %** reduction, where the recommendation's premise implies near-elimination.
   * discretisation error at `dx = 12` against the power-law limit goes **+0.44 → +1.67 %**, **+1.76 → +1.57 %**, **+1.05 → +1.51 %**: unchanged to slightly worse.

   **Stated in the words the brief asks for: on the GBM tissue ladders the normaliser is NOT the dominant term, and the p99 audit's central claim is refuted on tissue.**

4. **Breast: the prediction is confirmed, and strongly.** Fine-rung span falls **41-84 %** (docetaxel **11.67 % → 1.82 %**, paclitaxel **11.09 % → 1.80 %**), and docetaxel's discretisation error at `dx = 12` collapses from **-5.62 % to +0.41 %** against the power-law limit — a **14x** reduction, and the specific number `p99_convention.md` §3.5 predicted would shrink. The two taxanes' anomalous opposite sign disappears with it.

5. **The reason for the split is `F_state`, and it is exactly the alternative explanation `results/boundary_layer_resolution.md` §6 offered.** The scored ratio factorises **exactly** as `f_pen x f_state x f_sens x interaction` (verified to <= 2.2e-16 at all 66 rungs), and `f_state` — the oxygen-banded cell-state resistance factor — **contains no exposure field and is untouched by any normaliser**. Its fine-rung span is **3.72 % on GBM** and **0.37 % on breast**: a **10x** difference between the two sections for the same statistic. Measured as its share of the variance of `ln(ratio)` over the fine rungs, `f_state` carries **25-51 % on GBM** and **0-11 % on breast**. **On GBM `F_state` alone (3.72 %) is already two thirds of what a perfect normaliser leaves behind (5.49-5.79 %). That is the honest headline: `tail90` cannot fix the GBM ladders no matter how good it is on a slab.**

6. **Two independent parts of the audit's mechanism also fail on tissue, both measured here.**
   * The identity `err(mean/N) = -err(N)/(1+err(N))` requires the mean to be grid-exact. On the slab it is (≤ 6.2e-06). **On tissue it is not**: the scored mean over the tumour-cell set moves **0.97-2.14 %** over the fine rungs and **2.27-4.35 %** over the full ladder — **more than three orders of magnitude** more (§6.1). The scored mean is a nearest-neighbour sample at a fixed cell set, not the domain mean, and the conservation identity does not protect it.
   * The "an order statistic is a step function of dx, a moment is smooth" argument (§3.1 of the audit) **inverts on tissue**: every convention the ladders carry — including `mean`, a moment — is **non-monotone on 9 of 9 drugs**, and the only two ladders on which `p99` is perfectly **monotone** (breast docetaxel and paclitaxel, 0 reversals) are the two where `p99` moves **most** (12.0 % and 11.5 % over the fine rungs). The audit's magnitude ranking does hold in the form that matters — both low-order conventions move less than both upper-tail order statistics on 9 of 9 (§6.2) — but the strict four-way ordering holds on only 2 of 9, and the monotonicity claim fails outright.

7. **What the audit got right, and it is a real result.** *Within* `f_pen` the normaliser is the dominant term on tissue, exactly as claimed: `p99`'s log-movement is **67-84 %** of the combined `ln(p99) + ln(mean)` movement over the fine rungs, on all nine drug/tissue pairs. The error in the recommendation is not that measurement — it is the step from "the normaliser dominates `f_pen`" to "the normaliser dominates the score", which skips `f_state`.

8. **Verdict consequences, computed last and reported separately (§7):** at `dx = 12 um`, `tail90` reproduces the published verdict set exactly — 8 PASS, 1 FAIL, the same drugs — independently reproducing `p99_convention.md` §5. But it changes the *character* of the one FAIL: breast docetaxel moves from **+1.5 %** above its band ceiling to **+35.2 %** above it, and on the counterfactual ladder it FAILs at **0 of 7** rungs instead of PASSing at 4 of 7. The "UNDETERMINED at dx = 12" reading in that file's §6.1 is a p99 artefact; under a better-behaved normaliser the FAIL is decisive.

**Recommendation (§8): NARROW the migration, do not drop it and do not proceed on the stated grounds.** `tail90` is still the better normaliser and the breast panel is the evidence, but the justification "it will make the tissue ladders interpretable" must be withdrawn — it is false on GBM. The GBM ladder's non-monotonicity is an `F_state` problem and belongs to whoever owns the oxygen/cell-state path.

## 2. What was measured instead, and why it bounds `tail90`

### 2.1 The exact factorisation

`kill_calibration.decompose`'s own recorded outputs satisfy

```
ratio_exact  =  f_penetration  x  f_state  x  f_sensitivity  x  interaction
```

Checked at **every one of the 66 spatial rungs** (24 GBM + 42 breast): worst relative deviation **2.22e-16** (one ulp). `f_sensitivity` is identically 1.0 on both panels (uniform drug sensitivity). So

```
ln ratio  =  ln f_pen  +  ln f_state  +  ln interaction
```

is an **exact additive split of the ladder**, and:

* **`f_state = mean(resist)` contains no exposure field at all.** It is the oxygen-banded cell-state resistance factor. **No choice of normaliser can change it by any amount.**
* `interaction = mean(p3*r)/(mean(p3)*mean(r))` is the normalised covariance between kill probability and resistance. It is weakly normaliser-dependent and carries **-3.6 % to +6.9 %** of the fine-rung variance and **-6.2 % to +9.6 %** of the full-ladder variance.
* `f_pen` is the only term the normaliser reaches.

### 2.2 The perfect-normaliser counterfactual

In the linear-Hill limit `f_pen -> mean(e)/N(e)`, so a normaliser with **exactly zero grid error** (`N(dx) = const`) gives `f_pen(dx) ∝ mean(dx)` and

```
ratio_perfect(dx)  ∝  mean(dx)^gamma  x  f_state(dx)  x  interaction(dx)
```

Every factor is measured at every rung. `gamma` is the Hill damping and is **not assumed**: it is the regression slope of the true `dln f_pen` on the surrogate `dln(mean/p99)` across each drug's own ladder. Fit quality, per drug: **R² = 0.978-0.9999**, worst residual **0.87 %**. It is cross-checked against the elasticity `dln f_pen / dln N` computed **exactly** on the real `dx = 12 um` per-cell field (§3), and against the two limiting values:

| drug | `gamma` (ladder regression) | `-dln f_pen/dln N` (exact, dx = 12) | difference | R² |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| gbm temozolomide | 1.1227 | 1.1355 | -1.1 % | 0.9996 |
| gbm cisplatin | 0.9814 | 0.8688 | +13.0 % | 0.9776 |
| gbm doxorubicin | 1.0465 | 0.9160 | +14.2 % | 0.9988 |
| breast doxorubicin / epirubicin | 0.9741 | 0.8642 | +12.7 % | 0.9999 |
| breast paclitaxel | 1.0516 | 0.9125 | +15.2 % | 0.9999 |
| breast docetaxel | 1.0531 | 0.9133 | +15.3 % | 0.9999 |
| breast 5-fluorouracil | 0.7908 | 0.7701 | +2.7 % | 0.9912 |
| breast cisplatin | 0.8221 | 0.7978 | +3.1 % | 0.9955 |

**And the conclusion does not depend on `gamma` at all.** Recomputed at the fitted value, at `gamma = 1` (exact linear Hill) and at `gamma = 0` (fully saturated, `f_pen` blind to the field), the monotonicity verdict is **identical in all 27 cases**: every counterfactual ladder is non-monotone at every `gamma`, with the same number of direction reversals to within one.

### 2.3 Why the counterfactual bounds `tail90` rather than merely resembling it

`tail90` is not perfect. Its own measured grid error on the controlled slab (`results/sweep/p99_gridslab.json`, `f_pen` at 1xIC50, vessel geometry, exchange OFF) is:

| dx (um) | 1 | 2 | 4 | 8 | **12** | 16 | 24 | 30 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **tail90** | -0.00 | -0.00 | -0.03 | -0.17 | **-0.08** | +0.05 | +0.41 | **+7.15** |
| p99 (shipped) | +0.43 | +0.94 | +1.89 | +4.48 | **+6.98** | +9.54 | +14.85 | +19.00 |

**Over the fine rungs the substitution is essentially exact: `|tail90| <= 0.17 %` at every slab rung with `dx <= 16 um`** (the slab ladder has no 18 um rung; 18 is bracketed by 16 um at +0.05 % and 24 um at +0.41 %). Since the GBM refutation lives entirely in the fine rungs, the counterfactual is faithful precisely where it matters. At `dx = 30 um` `tail90` breaks down (+7.15 %) and the counterfactual is optimistic there; all-rung numbers below are flagged accordingly.

**The logic of the bound.** The counterfactual gives the normaliser the maximum benefit of the doubt available in principle. If the counterfactual ladder is still non-monotone with a comparable spread, then no normaliser — `tail90`, `tail95`, `mean`, or anything else — can produce a well-behaved ladder, because the residual is normaliser-free by construction.

### 4.3 The verdict on the diagnosis, stated plainly

It does not collapse; it goes to 5.5-5.8 % and gets rougher.

>
> *This file is hand-maintained. Despite the header above claiming "Generated by `experiments/tail90_falsification.py`", that script writes only JSON to `results/sweep/` and emits no markdown, so this note is durable. Full analysis: `results/fstate_mechanism.md`.*

> **Everything measured in this section is correct and is independently reproduced** — `results/fstate_mechanism.md` §3 measures a production `F_state` fine-rung span of **3.65 %** against the **3.72 %** recorded here, on the same ladder.
>
> **The attribution above is half right, and it was the closest anyone got.** *"Something dx-dependent in the reconstruction dominates"* (`results/boundary_layer_resolution.md` §6) is **correct** — it is the reconstruction. But the mechanism everyone then assumed, perivascular under-resolution of the oxygen boundary layer, is **wrong**: `results/subgrid_closure.md` §2 measures the shipped oxygen solve's worst discretisation error at **0.170 % at `dx = 30 um`**.
>
> The dominant term is the **binary vessel-source rasterisation on the write side**. `discrete_vascular_field(geometry='binary')` paints a cell as vessel iff its centre lies within 7.5 um of an endothelial cell, so the **total painted vessel area is not conserved under `dx`** (**4.73 %** span, non-monotone). Decomposed as `cov(term, F_state)/var(F_state)`: **source rasterisation +1.057**, PDE discretisation **−0.056**, cell-to-grid sampling **−0.001**. Holding the oxygen field completely fixed and varying only how cells read it reproduces just **1.3 %** of the movement.
>
> **This strengthens rather than weakens §6.1's conclusion.** "No normaliser touches it" is right, and now for a stated reason: the term is not a numerical error in the transport solve that a better normaliser could absorb — it enters upstream, in how much oxygen source is painted, and is then amplified ~6.7x by the fact that **10.10 %** of tumour cells sit within 0.01 of the `0.4` band edge. **The GBM-vs-breast contrast this section rests on is therefore also explained**: it is not that GBM's boundary layer is harder to resolve, but that GBM's tumour population is piled against a hard band edge (91.27 % below the ceiling, whole population spanning only O2 0.2924–0.4237) where breast's is not.



**The bound this puts on any normaliser change.** On GBM, `F_state` alone gives a fine-rung span of **3.72 %** with 4 of 5 direction reversals. A perfect normaliser leaves **5.49-5.79 %** with 4 of 5 reversals. So of the perfect-normaliser residual, roughly two thirds is `F_state` and the rest is the tumour-cell mean exposure (**1.37-1.48 %** fine-rung span) plus the exposure/state covariance (**0.32-0.59 %**) — **all three of them normaliser-free**.

### 6.2 Two further parts of the mechanism that do not transfer

**(a) The mean is not grid-exact on tissue, and the identity rests on it.** `p99_convention.md` §1.4 derives `err(mean/N) = -err(N)/(1+err(N))` from the domain mean being grid-exact with exchange OFF, measured at **≤ 6.2e-06** on the slab. The quantity that enters the tissue score is not the domain mean but the mean over a **fixed tumour-cell set that nearest-neighbour samples the grid field**. Measured:

| tissue | drug | `mean(e)` span, fine rungs | `mean(e)` span, all rungs | monotone? |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| gbm | temozolomide | 1.37 % | 3.82 % | no (4 reversals) |
| gbm | cisplatin | 1.43 % | 3.96 % | no (4 reversals) |
| gbm | doxorubicin | 1.48 % | 4.35 % | no (3 reversals) |
| breast | docetaxel | 2.14 % | 4.06 % | no (1 reversal) |
| breast | paclitaxel | 2.12 % | 4.02 % | no (1 reversal) |
| breast | doxorubicin / epirubicin | 1.55 % | 3.23 % | no (1 reversal) |
| breast | cisplatin | 1.12 % | 2.63 % | no (2 reversals) |
| breast | 5-fluorouracil | 0.97 % | 2.27 % | no (2 reversals) |

**More than three orders of magnitude larger than the slab figure (a factor of 1,600 to 7,000), and non-monotone on 9 of 9.** The conservation identity is a statement about the finite-volume operator's row sums over the *domain*; it says nothing about a subset average read at fixed physical positions, and the tissue mask and vessel painting both move with `dx`. **On tissue the normaliser is not the whole grid-error budget even within `f_pen`** — it is 67-84 % of it (§6.3), with the mean supplying the rest.

**(b) The "step function of dx" argument inverts on tissue.** `p99_convention.md` §3.1 explains the ladders' non-monotonicity by an order statistic being a step function of `dx` while moments are smooth. The ladder records carry four normalisers at every rung, so this is directly checkable. Fine-rung span, and whether the ladder is monotone:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| gbm | temozolomide | 1.37 %, no | 1.49 %, no | 6.16 %, no | 8.14 %, no |
| gbm | cisplatin | 1.43 %, no | 1.35 %, no | 7.50 %, no | 14.06 %, no |
| gbm | doxorubicin | 1.48 %, no | 1.12 %, no | 4.84 %, no | 19.28 %, no |
| breast | docetaxel | 2.14 %, no | 5.59 %, no | **12.04 %, MONOTONE** | 10.96 %, no |
| breast | paclitaxel | 2.12 %, no | 5.28 %, no | **11.45 %, MONOTONE** | 10.96 %, no |
| breast | doxorubicin / epirubicin | 1.55 %, no | 1.53 %, no | 3.49 %, no | 11.28 %, no |
| breast | cisplatin | 1.12 %, no | 0.87 %, no | 2.28 %, no | 2.50 %, no |

* **The magnitude ordering survives in the form that matters.** Both low-order conventions are smaller than both upper-tail order statistics on **9 of 9** (`max(mean, p50) < min(p99, max)` everywhere), which is the audit's ranking claim. The strict four-way ordering `mean <= p50 <= p99 <= max` holds on only **2 of 9** — `p50` beats `mean` on four drugs, and on the two taxanes `p99` moves MORE than `max` (12.04 % vs 10.96 %, 11.45 % vs 10.96 %), which no step-function argument predicts.
* **The monotonicity claim does not survive.** Every convention, *including the moment*, is non-monotone on 9 of 9 drugs. And the only two ladders on which `p99` is perfectly monotone are the two on which it moves **most** (12.04 %, 11.45 %). A step-function artefact is not what is driving these ladders; a real, smooth, under-resolved boundary layer is (docetaxel `L = 28.2 um` = 2.4 cells at `dx = 12 um`), with a separate rough term from the reconstruction on top.

### 6.3 The part of the audit that is confirmed

Within `f_pen`, the normaliser IS the dominant term on tissue, exactly as claimed. Share of the combined `ln(p99) + ln(mean)` log-movement that belongs to `p99`, over the fine rungs:

| gbm tmz | gbm cis | gbm dox | breast doc | breast pac | breast dox/epi | breast 5fu | breast cis |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.815 | 0.836 | 0.763 | 0.843 | 0.838 | 0.691 | 0.741 | 0.670 |

**67-84 % on all nine.** The audit's measurement is right. What fails is the inference from it: `f_pen` is not the score, and on GBM `f_state` is a term of comparable size that no normaliser can reach.

### 6.4 A structural finding: the normaliser's grid error partly CANCELS `F_state`'s on GBM

Correlation over the fine rungs between the normaliser's contribution to `ln(ratio)` (which is `-ln N`) and `ln f_state`. Negative means the two grid errors partially cancel, so **removing the normaliser makes the ladder worse**:

## 8. Recommendation

**Narrow the migration. Do not proceed on the stated grounds, and do not drop it.**

1. **Withdraw the claim that changing the normaliser will make the tissue dx ladders interpretable.** `p99_convention.md` §7.1 says the change "removes the only step-function term … which is the precondition for the tissue ladders having an order of accuracy at all", and §3.5 calls that "the concrete, falsifiable payoff of the change". **Measured: on GBM it is false in all three of its parts, and even on breast the improved ladders still have no extractable order** (both taxanes' power-law fits go from non-degenerate to degenerate and their Richardson triples from usable to unusable). Neither panel gains an order of accuracy.

2. **`tail90` remains the better normaliser, and the case for it is the case §3.1-§3.4 of that file already makes** — 85x smaller grid error on the controlled problem, half the bootstrap variance, no half-sample bias, a defensible physical referent — plus the breast result measured here: the 41-84 % span reduction and the 14x collapse of docetaxel's discretisation error are real and are the largest verified benefit anywhere in either file. **That is a sufficient case. It just is not the case that was made.**

3. **If the migration is done, redirect its stated payoff.** The honest claim is: *"`tail90` removes the dominant grid-error term from the breast panel's short-`L` drugs, where it is 84 % of the fine-rung movement, and reduces the GBM panel's by 12-26 %. It does not make either ladder monotone or give either an order of accuracy, because the oxygen-banded `F_state` term is non-monotone on GBM with a 3.72 % fine-rung span and no normaliser touches it."*

4. **The GBM ladder's non-monotonicity is an `F_state` problem and should be routed as one.** It belongs to whoever owns the oxygen solve and the cell-state banding, not to the scoring convention. The concrete target: the GBM quiescent fraction moves 0.8996-0.9390 across `dx = 7-18 um` (`results/grid_convergence_gbm.md` §5) and `F_state` moves 3.72 % with it. That, not the normaliser, is what a GBM grid-convergence study is currently measuring.

5. **Fix the cheap thing first: persist the ladder exposure fields.** Both ladder scripts already compute `e_unit` per rung and hold it in memory. Writing it would make this test — and every future convention question — genuinely free, and would let `tail90` be measured on the ladders directly instead of bounded. **Until then, the direct test named in `p99_convention.md` §8 costs a 1.5-2.5 h re-run of both ladders, not zero.**

6. **`p99_convention.md` §6.1's UNDETERMINED downgrade of breast docetaxel is correct under p99 and should be recorded as convention-specific** (§7.2). Under `tail90` that verdict is a decisive FAIL. (That file is owned by another workstream — reported here, not edited.)

## 9. What this report does NOT establish

1. **`tail90` was not measured on the tissue ladders.** It was **bounded** by a normaliser with exactly zero grid error, which no real convention can beat. The bound is tight over the fine rungs (`tail90`'s own slab error is ≤ 0.17 % for `dx ≤ 18 um`) and loose at `dx = 30 um` (+7.15 %), so all-rung numbers flatter `tail90` and fine-rung numbers do not. **The GBM refutation rests entirely on the fine rungs and is therefore the safe half; the breast confirmation's full-ladder figures are the flattered half and the fine-rung figures are quoted alongside them.** A direct measurement would still be worth having and now has a stated price.

2. **The counterfactual assumes the non-normaliser terms respond to `dx` the same way under either convention.** `f_state` and `f_sensitivity` are exactly normaliser-free, so this is exact for them. `interaction` is not — it is weakly normaliser-dependent — but it carries only **-6.2 % to +9.6 %** of the ladder variance, so the assumption's reach is bounded by that. The `mean(e)` term is exactly normaliser-free.

3. **`gamma` is a fitted quantity.** It is fitted per drug at R² = 0.978-0.9999, cross-checked against an exact elasticity computed on the real `dx = 12` field (agreement within 15 %), and the monotonicity conclusions are unchanged at `gamma = 0` and `gamma = 1`. It is nevertheless a fit and is labelled as one.

4. **Nothing here re-solves any transport equation**, so the exposure fields, the `alpha` calibration, the literature bands and the published verdicts are all exactly as they were. This measures; it does not migrate. `tmesim/validation/kill_calibration.py`, `BATH_REFERENCE_PCT` and every shipped default are untouched.

5. **`F_state`'s grid dependence is diagnosed, not fixed, and its magnitude is read from the published ladder records** rather than re-derived from the oxygen solve. Whether the GBM section's 3.72 % swing is a genuine physical sensitivity or a banding-threshold artefact is a question for the oxygen/cell-state workstream and is not answered here.

6. **One section per tissue.** The 10x difference in `F_state` grid sensitivity between the GBM and breast sections is measured on one section each. Whether it is a property of GBM tissue or of this GBM section is untested, and it is the single assumption the §4-versus-§5 split rests on.

7. **A better-behaved ladder is not a validated model.** `results/kill_calibration.md` §3 and `results/grid_convergence_gbm.md` §8.6 both already say this, and changing the normalising convention does not touch any of it: the literature bands are wide, the 2-D reference has a ~19x IQR, and the cell-state multipliers this file has just shown to be load-bearing are themselves unvalidated priors.
