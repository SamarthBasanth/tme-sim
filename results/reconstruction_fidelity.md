# Reconstruction fidelity — rigorous validation on REAL data

Generated by `tmesim/validation/reconstruction_fidelity.py`. Every number below comes from REAL measured data (10x Xenium GBM, 10x Visium GBM, the Darmanis GSE84465 scRNA-seq atlas, Ivy GAP anatomic-structure RNA-seq). There is no synthetic ground truth anywhere in these checks.

## 1. Cross-platform composition concordance

Two independent real platforms + two independent estimators asked for the same GBM composition. Xenium: 40,887 single cells, threshold-gated marker typing. Visium: 3,468 spots, NNLS deconvolution vs the Darmanis atlas. Fractions renormalised over the four shared classes.

| cell type | Xenium (gated) | Visium (NNLS) | abs. diff |
|---|---|---|---|
| TUMOR | 73.80% | 82.66% |  8.86% |
| STROMAL |  7.92% |  1.04% |  6.88% |
| IMMUNE |  8.54% |  6.53% |  2.01% |
| ENDOTHELIAL |  9.74% |  9.77% |  0.03% |

- Mean absolute difference across the 4 classes: ** 4.44%**
- Pearson r of the 4-vector: **0.997**
- Pearson r of the non-tumour subvector (harder test): **0.945**

> Xenium = 319-gene TARGETED panel, single cells, threshold-gated marker typing (hard-assigns each cell). Visium = whole-transcriptome spots, NNLS deconvolution to a soft fraction against the Darmanis atlas. Different assays + different estimators, so exact equality is NOT expected; concordance of the ORDERING and rough magnitudes is the real test. NECROTIC excluded (neither method assigns it).

## 2. Region-marker validation (effect size + permutation p-value)

Sample `10x_parent_gbm` (3,468 spots; 283 shared signature genes). Region labels from `region_label.label_regions` (real Ivy GAP signatures). Marker expression is per-spot log1p CP10k. p-values are one-sided label-permutation tests.

Region label distribution: core=786, margin=90, necrotic=1628, perivascular=964

| test | n(region) | mean in | mean rest | Cohen's d | perm p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PECAM1 in perivascular | 964 | 0.829 | 0.210 | 1.29 | 1.00e-04 |
| CA9 in necrotic | 1628 | 0.224 | 0.026 | 0.67 | 1.00e-04 |
| VEGFA in necrotic | 1628 | 2.563 | 1.306 | 1.35 | 1.00e-04 |

Interpretation: a positive Cohen's d with a small p-value means the region label is genuinely marker-faithful (perivascular really is endothelial-rich; necrotic really is hypoxia-marker-rich), not an artefact of the labeller.

## 4. Honest fidelity verdict — what this DOES and DOES NOT establish

**These checks DO establish:**
- The reconstruction is **cross-platform self-consistent**: two independent real assays + estimators agree on the composition ordering (tumour-dominant, minority immune/endothelial/stromal) and rough magnitudes.
- The niche-region labels are **marker-faithful**: perivascular spots are significantly PECAM1-high and necrotic spots significantly CA9/VEGFA-high (positive effect sizes, permutation p-values), so the anatomic zones reflect real biology rather than labeller artefacts.
- The deconvolution is **stable** to which reference cells are used (small subsampling spread) and **recovers known mixtures** of held-out real cells with low error — the NNLS estimator is sound.

**These checks DO NOT establish:**
- They do **NOT** validate drug-response / kill prediction. This open section carries **no paired patient outcome label**; the per-cell `sensitivity` is a documented PROVISIONAL placeholder, not a measured response (see `docs/REAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` L4 and the Xenium reconstructor docstring).
- Cross-platform agreement is **concordance, not ground truth**: both platforms could share a bias (e.g. the shared Darmanis reference, panel gene selection). Absolute composition is not independently certified.
- Pseudobulk recovery validates the **estimator**, using real cells mixed at a known answer; it is not a claim about any real tissue's composition.

Bottom line: the REAL reconstruction is validated as **self-consistent, cross-platform-concordant, and marker-faithful**. Drug-response fidelity remains **access-gated / unproven** and is honestly out of scope here.
