# Vascular-KDE bandwidth sweep — 10 µm

One data point in a 20-point sweep of `real_fields._kde_on_grid`'s Gaussian bandwidth (production default 40 µm, `mode='kde'`). Built `vascular_density_from_cells(domain, cells, bandwidth_um=10.0, mode='kde')` in-memory on `reconstruct_real('xenium', dx_um=12)`; a matching in-memory `bandwidth_um=40.0` field was built alongside it as the genuine KDE comparator (see note below on why `vascular_validation.md`'s own published field numbers cannot serve that role). No existing file edited; script and raw JSON kept in the scratchpad only.

**Bandwidth cannot move the diagnostic it was proposed to fix, and the "40 µm-default" comparator this task assumed does not currently exist as published output.** `vascular_validation.measure()`'s CSR-ratio and heterogeneity tests are computed directly from raw endothelial-cell coordinates via DBSCAN vessel-clustering and 500 µm tile-binning — never touching `vascular_density` or its bandwidth — so bandwidth=10 µm reproduces the published 0.99/"1.31×"/111/2.4× numbers exactly (to the extent they're reproducible: recomputing the tail ratio directly via `measure()` gives 1.439×, not the "1.31×" quoted in `vascular_validation.md`'s prose, which appears to use a different tail statistic — flagging, not resolving, that discrepancy here). Separately: `reconstruct_real`'s vascular field has defaulted to `mode='discrete'` since `results/vessel_promotion.md`, so `vascular_validation.md`'s own field-derived numbers (its "40 µm-bandwidth Gaussian KDE" docstring notwithstanding) and `results/real_drug_panel.md`/`delivery_metrics.md`'s transport numbers are built on the discrete segmented-vessel source, **not** a KDE at any bandwidth — this run's `bandwidth=40` in-memory build is therefore the first genuine same-pipeline KDE comparator on file. Against that real comparator, dropping bandwidth 40→10 µm shrinks the field's nonzero footprint from 99.8% to 54.8% of tissue and its downstream mean tumour AUC by ~4×, while barely moving tile-level field heterogeneity (2.78×→2.98×, +7%) — i.e. the field gets more concentrated and delivers far less drug, but does not get meaningfully more *heterogeneous* at the tile scale, so 10 µm alone does not close the gap to the real CD34 clustering spread (0.35–16.89%, ~48×). The field itself is numerically sane at bandwidth=10 (no NaN, proper [0,1] range, not degenerate), and the dx=20 rough cisplatin transport check ran without incident.
