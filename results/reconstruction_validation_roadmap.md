# Reconstruction validation roadmap — does the tissue itself look real?

**Scope, stated once, up front, because it is easy to conflate with a different question this project has already answered.** `results/DATA_HUNT.md` closed the question "can we validate predicted drug RESPONSE against measured drug response, in space, for any cancer" — the answer is no, for any cancer, anywhere in open data, and closing that gap needs a clinical/lab partnership. **That is not this document.** This document is about a smaller, already-partially-answered, fully achievable question: **does the RECONSTRUCTED MICROENVIRONMENT — vasculature, ECM, cell composition, oxygen field — resemble real tissue biology, checked against published morphometry?** No wet lab, no partnership, no new data acquisition. Literature search and, in several cases, just running code that already exists against data that is already on disk.

**The framing that motivates ranking things the way this document ranks them.** The project's own re-framing (see `results/SESSION_AUDIT.md` §7, and the cross-platform-concordance retraction it documents) is the MD-force-field analogy: validate the underlying physical laws and parameters against a benchmark suite once, then trust downstream predictions built on them, without re-certifying every case. Under that analogy, **reconstruction-level checks are the force-field parameterization, not the production run** — they are necessary and genuinely informative, but they are not the same kind of evidence as a transport/physics-level check, and this project's own history says so directly: §5 below states why, with receipts.

Date of this pass: 2026-07-28. Every claim below carries a PMID or is explicitly marked `UNVERIFIED — search lead only`. Nothing here was invented to fill a table cell.

### 1.1 What this catalogue says as a whole

Nine of twelve rows are checkable-and-checked; six of those replicate or partially replicate (1, 2, 8, 9-partially, 10-partially, 11), five fail outright or on at least one section (3-s2, 4, 5, 6, 7). No row is unambiguously clean across every section tested. **The pattern that should be carried forward, not re-argued:** every quantity here that is a *point-pattern statistic derived from cell positions* (fraction, MVD, spacing, clustering) is fragile and section-dependent; the one quantity that is a *stereological identity requiring no cell-type call at all* (row 8, vessel profile density) is the most robust. That is a methodological lesson for §6, not just a GBM finding.

## 2. GBM — checkable now, not yet checked

Ranked by how load-bearing a positive or negative result would be.

### 2.1 STROMAL cell fraction vs published GBM stromal/fibroblast content — HIGH VALUE, MODERATE EFFORT

The reconstruction reports STROMAL at 11.78% (s1) / 15.37% (s2) / (s3 not reported in the read files) of all cells, gated on brain-lineage astrocytic markers (MOG/AQP4/GJA1 on s1, SLC1A3/S100B/PTN on s2 — **note these are astrocyte/glial markers, not fibroblast/CAF markers**, because GBM's "stroma" is mostly reactive astrocytes and neuropil, not classic mesenchymal stroma). This has never been checked against anything published.

**What to search for:** GBM does not have a curated "% astrocyte/glial stroma of tumour cellularity" literature the way breast has a curated "% fibroblastic stroma" one — the closest anchors are (a) single-cell/snRNA-seq GBM atlases reporting non-malignant glial-cell proportions (e.g. Neftel 2019 4-state GBM atlas, Darmanis 2017 single-cell GBM), and (b) IHC-based reactive astrogliosis quantification at the tumour margin. `UNVERIFIED — search lead only`: Neftel CH et al., *Cell* 2019;178(4):835-849, PMID [31327527](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31327527/) reports malignant-vs-non-malignant cell fractions per tumour by scRNA-seq; this is the natural first anchor but carries the **same dissociation-bias caveat** already established for the endothelial fraction in `results/classifier_fix.md` and `results/vascular_validation.md` §3 — a dissociated-cell assay and an intact-tissue profile-count assay do not share a denominator, and this project has already shown that mismatch alone can produce a multi-fold apparent discrepancy that is not biological.

### 2.2 IMMUNE cell fraction vs published GBM immune infiltrate — HIGH VALUE, NEEDS A DENOMINATOR-MATCHED SOURCE

Reconstructed IMMUNE fraction is 19.48% (s1) / 11.39% (s2), gated on PTPRC/CD68/AIF1/CD163 (pan-leukocyte + myeloid). Two classes of published anchor exist and **only one of them is a fair comparator**:

- **Flow cytometry / scRNA-seq on dissociated GBM tissue** — Klemm F et al., *Cell* 2020;181(7):1643-1660, PMID [32470396](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32470396/), and Müller S et al., *Genome Biol* 2017;18(1):234, PMID [29262845](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29262845/), both report myeloid/CD45+ fractions in the 30–50%+ range of *live dissociated cells*. **Do not use these as the primary comparator without the same denominator correction §1's row 1/10 already required for endothelial cells** — dissociation enriches for immune cells relative to intact-tissue nuclear profile counts (differential dissociation efficiency, immune-cell survival bias), and this project has already demonstrated (rows 1 and 10 above) that a naive cross-modality ratio can be wrong by an order of magnitude for reasons that are pure denominator mismatch, not biology. Citing 30–50% against this project's 11–19% without that correction would repeat a mistake this project has already caught itself making once.
- **The correct comparator is an intact-tissue, profile-count-based estimate** — IHC (Iba1/CD68/CD3) cell density or % on GBM FFPE sections, or a spatial-transcriptomics deconvolution reporting immune cell *proportions* rather than dissociated-tissue proportions. `UNVERIFIED — search lead only`: a preliminary pass found Sørensen MD et al., *CNS Oncol* 2018;7(2):CNS21, PMID [29513026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29513026/) ("Distribution of Tumor-Infiltrating Immune Cells in Glioblastoma") as a candidate IHC-based quantification, and a separate report of CD68 density near 9.1% in one GBM IHC series — **neither number was independently confirmed or extracted from the primary source in this pass; both need direct verification against the paper before citing a number.**

### 2.3 ECM / collagen content — the GBM-is-NOT-desmoplastic contrast point

**Qualitatively well established, quantitatively unresolved.** Payne LS & Huang PH, *Mol Cancer Res* 2013;11(10):1129-1140, PMID [23861322](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23861322/): "levels of collagens in the normal adult brain are low"; fibrillar ECM is atypical of brain parenchyma generally and largely restricted to perivascular basement membrane, in contrast to the dense fibrillar-collagen stroma of systemic carcinomas. GBM does show elevated collagen relative to normal brain as part of tumour progression, but starts from — and by every qualitative account stays well below — the desmoplastic reaction seen in breast, pancreatic, or colorectal carcinoma.

This project's own s1 reconstruction already shows the panel-content signature of that fact obliquely: s1 carries 11 ECM/collagen marker genes (COL1A2, COL12A1, COL25A1, POSTN, VCAN, DCN, FBLN1, LAMA2, THBS1, SPON1, TGFBI) and s2 only 3 (VCAN, DCN, FBLN1, missing both fibrillar-collagen markers COL1A2 and POSTN) — `heldout_patient.md` §3.5 already flags that mean ECM density (0.076 vs 0.457) is **not comparable across the two panels as biology**, purely a marker-count artefact. That same warning applies before ANY absolute ECM number from this reconstruction is quoted against a published collagen measurement: the ECM field is a max-normalised KDE, exactly the construction that produced the now-well-documented grid-dependent normalisation problem for the vascular field (`vessel_promotion.md` §2.5). **Do not attempt an absolute GBM ECM-density-vs-published-collagen-% comparison until the ECM field has the same discrete/measured-unit treatment the vascular field already got.** What IS checkable cheaply: a purely qualitative direction check — does the reconstruction's ECM field concentrate near vessels/tumour margin the way perivascular basement-membrane collagen should, rather than being uniform? `UNVERIFIED — search lead only` for a quantitative GBM collagen area-fraction reference: search "picrosirius red collagen area fraction glioblastoma quantification" and "second harmonic generation collagen glioma."

## 3. Breast cancer — from scratch (nothing has been checked yet)

`results/breast_cancer_flagship.md` reconstruction, real Xenium Rep1 (Janesick et al. 2023, PMID [38114474](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38114474/), 167,780 cells, 313-gene panel): TUMOR 53.5%, STROMAL 22.9%, IMMUNE 15.4%, ENDOTHELIAL 8.2%, mean vascular density 0.057, mean ECM density 0.242. Tumour is annotated ER+/HER2+/PR− in the source paper. **None of these five numbers has been checked against a published measurement.** Ranked by achievability × how load-bearing a result would be.

### 3.1 Microvessel density — HIGHEST VALUE, LOWEST EFFORT, DO THIS FIRST

**This is close to a free action.** The breast Xenium panel carries **13 endothelial marker genes** (PECAM1, VWF, CLDN5, CLEC14A, EGFL7, KDR, RAMP2, MMRN2, SOX17, SOX18, NOSTRIN, AQP1, ESM1, ANGPT2) against GBM s1's single PECAM1 — a richer vascular readout than either GBM section has ever had. `tmesim/reconstruct/vessel_network.py`, the exact single-linkage discrete-vessel segmentation already run on GBM in `vessel_network_impact.md` and promoted to default in `vessel_promotion.md`, has **not been run on the breast reconstruction at all** — `breast_cancer_flagship.md` reports only the raw KDE-style mean vascular density field (0.057), never a discrete vessel count or an MVD/mm². Running the existing, unmodified segmentation function against the existing, already-loaded breast endothelial cells is the single cheapest, highest-value action in this entire roadmap.

**What to compare it against, and the caveat that must travel with the number.** A quick search pass did **not** find a breast-cancer entry in the same whole-slide, unbiased-systematic-random-sampling MVD study already anchoring the GBM number (Marien 2016, PMID 27583442 covers only colorectal, GBM, ovarian, renal — confirmed by direct check, breast is absent). The classic Weidner hotspot-method breast studies exist (mean/median microvessel counts ~60/56 per 0.74 mm² field, i.e. ~75–81/mm² — Weidner N et al., *J Natl Cancer Inst* 1992;84(24):1875-1887; `UNVERIFIED — PMID not independently confirmed in this pass`) but **hotspot counting is deliberately biased high relative to whole-slide sampling**, and this project's own GBM anchor was explicitly chosen (`vascular_validation.md` §2) to avoid exactly that bias. Using a hotspot number as the comparator for a whole-tissue reconstructed field would be comparing incompatible statistics — the same class of error the project caught itself on the s2 area-fraction row (#3 in §1). `UNVERIFIED — search lead only`, the concrete next task: search specifically for a **whole-slide or systematic-uniform-random-sampled** CD31/CD34 breast MVD study — the methodology keyword to search on is "unbiased counting" or "systematic uniform random sampling" combined with "breast carcinoma microvessel density," not just "Weidner."

### 3.2 Stromal / desmoplastic content — the real GBM-vs-breast contrast, HIGH VALUE

Breast IS classically desmoplastic — unlike GBM (§2.3) — so this is the sharpest available cross-cancer sanity check in the whole roadmap: **if the reconstruction is tracking real biology, breast STROMAL fraction should sit meaningfully higher, relative to any comparable published baseline, than GBM's does.** (Naively it already does — 22.9% vs GBM's 11.8–15.4% — but that comparison uses two *different* marker gates on two different tissues' own definition of "stromal," so it is suggestive, not yet a validated result.)

**Candidate reference**, found and partially checked in this pass: Dekker TJ et al. / a digital-image-analysis quantification of myofibroblastic (α-SMA+) stromal reaction across 154 resected invasive breast carcinoma-NST specimens, *PLOS ONE* 2019;14(1):e0210263 — `UNVERIFIED — PMID not independently confirmed`, level 0–46% of tissue area, median 15.1%, SD 7.5, higher in grade 2–3 tumours. **The unit mismatch that must be stated, not glossed over:** this is an **IHC area-fraction** of α-SMA+ myofibroblastic stroma specifically, not a **cell-count fraction** of all stromal-lineage cells (LUM/POSTN/PDGFRA-gated, per this project's breast marker map) — the same class of area-vs-cell-fraction mismatch that made the vascular-area-fraction row (§1, row 3) the one quantity in the whole GBM catalogue that could be scored cleanly, because it was compared to another AREA fraction, not against a cell count. Comparing this project's STROMAL *cell fraction* against a stromal *area fraction* would need an explicit conversion or an honestly-flagged apples-to-oranges caveat; a cleaner second search target is a study reporting **stromal CELL fraction** (e.g. from a spatial transcriptomics deconvolution of breast tumours, which would share the project's own denominator) rather than an IHC area score.

### 3.3 Tumour-infiltrating lymphocytes (TILs) — well-studied, clinically standardised, HIGH VALUE

Breast cancer has the best-developed reference framework of anything in this roadmap: Salgado R et al., *Ann Oncol* 2015;26(2):259-271, PMID [25214542](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25214542/) (the International TILs Working Group 2014) defines the standardised visual scoring method and the low(<10%)/intermediate(10–39%)/high(≥40%) stromal-TIL bands now used clinically worldwide. Subtype pattern is well replicated across many follow-on studies: TNBC and HER2+ tumours run toward the higher end, ER+/HER2− toward the lower end. **This specific tumour is ER+/HER2+/PR−** — a mixed profile, so the applicable comparator band is not immediately obvious from the subtype literature and needs its own targeted search (`UNVERIFIED — search lead only`: median/IQR stromal-TIL% specifically reported for ER+/HER2+ cohorts, e.g. in neoadjuvant HER2-targeted-therapy trial biomarker papers).

**The unit mismatch to state up front, because it is larger here than anywhere else in this document.** The clinical TILs score is (a) an **area-based visual estimate**, (b) restricted to the **stromal compartment only** (mononuclear cells occupying stromal area between tumour nests, explicitly excluding intratumoral/intra-nest lymphocytes and excluding the tumour area itself from the denominator), and (c) **lymphocyte-specific**, not pan-leukocyte. This project's reconstructed IMMUNE fraction (15.4%) is a **cell-count fraction of the WHOLE tissue** gated on PTPRC/CD68/AIF1/CD163 — pan-leukocyte, includes macrophages, and is not restricted to the stromal compartment. A naive "15.4% vs the 10–39% intermediate band" comparison would look like agreement and would be measuring two different things by three separate axes at once (area vs. cell count, whole-tissue vs. stromal-only denominator, pan-leukocyte vs. lymphocyte-only numerator). **The achievable version of this check is narrower and should be scoped as such**: restrict the reconstructed immune cells to the STROMAL-typed region only, and — if the panel supports separating lymphocyte markers (PTPRC alone, or a T/B-lineage subset) from myeloid markers (CD68/AIF1/CD163) — score the lymphocyte-only, stromal-restricted fraction against the Salgado band. That is a real, scoped, achievable task; the un-scoped version is not a fair test.

### 3.4 ER / HER2 / PR receptor-positive cell fraction — LOWER VALUE, MODALITY CAVEAT IS SEVERE

ESR1 and ERBB2 are confirmed present and measurable on this panel (`breast_cancer_flagship.md` §1a); this specific tumour is clinically annotated ER+/HER2+/PR−. Allred-score clinical convention: ER-positive requires ≥1% nuclear-positive cells by IHC; "high" ER expression (Allred 5) corresponds to ≥67% of tumour cells positive — `UNVERIFIED — search lead only`, from Allred DC et al.'s original scoring scheme, cross-checked here only via secondary sources, not the primary paper.

**Why this is the weakest check in the whole roadmap, not just an under-resolved one.** IHC ER positivity is a **protein, nuclear-localisation** assay; Xenium ESR1 detection is an **mRNA, whole-cell transcript-count** assay. This project has already demonstrated, in its own GBM hypoxia work, that mRNA and protein readouts of the *same* gene can diverge for a specific, named, mechanistic reason — `heldout_patient.md` §4.3 shows HIF1A mRNA is uncorrelated with hypoxia (rank 327/480) precisely *because* HIF-1α is regulated post-translationally, not transcriptionally, and calls this "expected on mechanism." ESR1/ER has its own well-known mRNA-protein discordance literature (post-transcriptional and epigenetic regulation of ER expression), so a quantitative ESR1-transcript-fraction vs. Allred-score comparison would be scientifically weaker than any other row in this document, not just harder to find data for. **The achievable version is a qualitative direction check only**: is ESR1 detected in a clear majority of TUMOR-typed cells (consistent with clinical ER+ status), and is ERBB2 detected broadly (consistent with clinical HER2+), rather than a quantitative percentage match. This should be reported, if done, as a sanity check that the panel content is internally consistent with the clinical annotation — not as validation of a receptor-positive cell fraction.

## 4. Not checkable without new data — and why this is a SMALL, different gap

Everything below is a genuine gap, but it is the "nobody happens to have published this specific number yet" kind of gap, not the "the data structurally doesn't exist anywhere" kind that `DATA_HUNT.md` documents for drug response. The distinction matters for how these should be reported: they are *incompleteness*, not *impossibility*.

- **3D vascular/ECM architecture.** Every check in §1–§3 is 2D, in-plane, single-section — `heldout_patient.md` §7 and `vascular_validation.md` §7 already flag this for GBM; `breast_cancer_flagship.md` §5 documents the same limit for breast plus the specific 3D-engine work that would be needed and the fact that even the registered two-section breast dataset (`registration_3d_breast_poc.md`) has only 2 z-planes, not a real volumetric stack. **A genuinely 3D morphometric comparison (e.g. against light-sheet or serial-block-face vascular reconstructions) is not achievable with today's data**, but it is a data-availability gap, not a structural one — multi-slice serial-section datasets exist for other tissue (e.g. DLPFC, already named as a candidate) and a cancer-specific one could plausibly turn up with more search.
- **Absolute field amplitudes.** The vascular KDE and ECM KDE fields are relative, max-normalised constructions with **no absolute physical scale** (`vessel_promotion.md` §7.2, `vessel_network_impact.md` §2). No published measurement can validate an amplitude that is defined only relative to the densest point in one section. This is not a missing-data gap at all — it is a **construction** choice, and the fix (already executed for vasculature via the discrete-vessel promotion) is to move to a source with a physically statable unit, not to search harder for a reference number.
- **Between-case variability at the population level.** Every check above is n = 1–3 sections. GBM CD34 vascular area alone spans 0.35–16.89% across published cases (48×, PMID 38928515). A single section — or three — cannot be expected to sit at a population mean, and no morphometric check in this document can distinguish "the reconstruction is biased" from "this particular section is unusual." This needs more sections, which is achievable (more public Xenium/Visium GBM and breast datasets exist and could be pulled in), not more literature search.

## 5. The standing caution, applied prospectively to everything in this document

This project's own audit trail (`results/SESSION_AUDIT.md` §4, §7) records that **every reconstruction-level external validation it has ever made has since been retracted or fails on a held-out section** — cross-platform concordance (0.997 → 0.980 on the classifier fix, then 0.016 on the non-tumour subvector), the endothelial fraction (replicates on 3/3 sections here, but only after two rounds of correcting the *comparison methodology itself* — the depth conversion and the denominator factorisation in `endothelial_density.md`), and the vascular area fraction (passes on s1, fails 5.23× on s2, `heldout_patient.md`). Meanwhile **every physics/transport-level validation has held**: the doxorubicin→mitoxantrone/topotecan out-of-sample penetration prediction (2/2, both defaults, `vessel_promotion.md` §4), the delivery×potency kill ordering (identical on both GBM sections, `heldout_patient.md` §3), the closed-form α calibration (field-independent by construction).

**That asymmetry should be read as a property of the two kinds of claim, not as bad luck**, and it applies to every new number this document generates, not just to what came before it:

1. A reconstruction-level check depends on classifier thresholds, marker-panel content, section thickness, and dissociation-vs-intact-tissue denominators — all of which this project has already shown can independently move a comparison by 2–35× (§1, rows 3, 4, 9, 10). A physics-level check (does penetration length scale correctly with tortuosity, does out-of-sample PK land in a cited band) depends on far fewer free choices and has, so far, never failed here.
2. **Any pass reported from this roadmap's §2/§3 tasks should be treated as REAL and USEFUL evidence that the reconstruction is not obviously wrong — and simultaneously as weaker evidence than any transport-level result already in `results/`.** A single-section morphometric agreement (as with the endothelial fraction before the held-out test) has already been shown, in this exact project, to look solid and then not replicate. Report new agreements with an explicit n and an explicit "this is one section" caveat, exactly as the existing GBM catalogue in §1 does — do not let a new breast MVD or TIL agreement get quoted as a headline the way the pre-retraction cross-platform 0.997 once was.
3. **A failure is more informative than a pass, and should be kept, not buried**, per the same logic `SESSION_AUDIT.md` §3 already used for the vascular clustering result (§1, row 7): "the reconstruction gets the vessel count roughly right and the arrangement wrong" was more useful to know than a clean pass would have been, because it named a specific, actionable defect (missing avascular zones) rather than a false reassurance.
