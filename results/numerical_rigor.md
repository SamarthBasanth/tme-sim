## Honest numerical verdict

1. **Conservation & stability are exact-to-round-off.** Mass is conserved on the real irregular mask to ~4e-14, concentrations stay non-negative, and the implicit scheme never destabilises across a 32× dt range. The *numerical method* is trustworthy on the real geometry.

2. **The prediction is dt-converged; kill is grid-converged on THIS section; the penetration index is not.** *(CORRECTED — this item previously said the penetration index "keeps falling" and is "over-estimated by ~15–25% at dx = 30 µm". It rises, and it is **under**-estimated by 26.6% there.)* In time, everything is invariant to <1e-2 for dt ≲ 20 s. In space, `kill_fraction` plateaus by dx ≲ 20 µm (≈0.295), while `penetration_index` keeps **rising** until at least dx ≈ 12–15 µm, because the ~45 µm perivascular boundary layer is unresolved on coarse grids. Quote penetration at dx ≲ 12 µm (≈0.306) and treat the 30 µm value as a **lower** bound. Note the scope limit: "kill is grid-converged" is measured **on this Xenium GBM section, for this metric**. It does **not** generalise — `results/grid_convergence_breast.md` finds the breast panel not converged at dx = 12 µm (docetaxel -3.4% against its Richardson limit, verdict flipping FAIL → PASS at dx ≥ 16 µm), and `results/grid_convergence_gbm.md` finds the GBM panel non-monotone in dx with no extractable order of accuracy.

3. **`grid_dx_um` is the top local driver of penetration; α is the top driver of kill and also the widest prior.** *(CORRECTED — this item previously named the endothelial typing threshold as the biggest penetration mover and the KDE bandwidth / grid dx as the biggest kill movers, and called α the smallest local elasticity. All of that is contradicted by §4's own table.)* Over a ±20% perturbation: for penetration, `grid_dx_um` **0.747** > α 0.450 > endothelial threshold 0.241 > KDE bandwidth 0.029; for kill, α **0.610** > endothelial threshold 0.517 > grid dx 0.127 > KDE bandwidth 0.010. So the limiting factors are **the mesh (for penetration) and the single-anchor calibration α (for kill)** — one numerical, one calibration — with the endothelial typing threshold a consistent second-tier contributor and the KDE bandwidth effectively inert. **It is not true that the limiting factor is biology/reconstruction rather than numerics.**

**Bottom line (CORRECTED).** The **solver** is numerically sound on the real microenvironment: mass-conservative to ~4e-14 on the measured irregular mask, non-negative, unconditionally stable, and dt-converged over a 32× range. Sections 1 and 3 stand as written and are untouched by this correction.

But **the mesh is not off the hook.** The previous bottom line ended *"the mesh is not the bottleneck once dx ≲ 12 µm"* — **that claim is withdrawn.** It rested on a misattributed elasticity: by §4's own table `grid_dx_um` is the single largest driver of the penetration index, at 3.1× the knob the prose credited. And it has since been tested directly and failed twice — `results/grid_convergence_breast.md` (breast panel not converged at dx = 12 µm; a verdict flips on the ladder) and `results/grid_convergence_gbm.md` (GBM panel non-monotone, no order of accuracy extractable). The mechanism both identify is that dx does not only enter the transport discretisation: the oxygen solve, the cell-state banding (`F_state`) and the IFP/velocity solve **all live on the grid**, so a converged transport step does not imply a converged prediction.

**What the error bars should actually be built from,** on this file's data: `grid_dx_um` and the single-anchor **α**, with the **endothelial typing threshold** second-tier and the **KDE bandwidth** negligible. And per-panel convergence must be *measured*, not inherited from this section — that is what the two grid-convergence reports exist to do.
