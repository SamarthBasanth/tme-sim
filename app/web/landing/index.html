02 — Transport physics

The shape is solved. The scale is not.

Convection, diffusion and reaction, solved on that geometry. Interstitial pressure builds where vessels leak and drainage fails, and pushes back against incoming drug; oxygen and hypoxia solve on the same grid; response follows Hill kinetics from the concentration each cell sees. What that buys is the shape of the field, not its magnitude — and the difference is measured, not asserted. One piece is exact rather than argued: how much drug the tissue can store is a number the engine assumes rather than measures, and it cannot move a converged field at all — checked to one part in a billion. It does move what a finite exposure window accumulates — the one regime the invariance does not cover.

03 — Delivery or potency

Which one killed the compound?

A compound compiles from its own structure: two routes through the cell layer, fed by lipophilicity and hydrogen-bond donors as well as mass, potency from measured GDSC2 screens. Structure reaches transport — scored unfitted on 68 measured compounds, but on descriptor columns supplied by that measurement, and the engine's own path has never been scored. It recovers about a quarter of the measured spread, so close analogues are under-separated, not resolved. Each run then decomposes survival cell by cell. On the flagship section 99.8% of surviving tumour cells are under-dosed, not unreached: the drug arrives almost everywhere and is almost everywhere too weak. A bench assay on that section could refute it; that is the point.

04 — Spatial attribution

The mean is not the finding. The spread is.

In one section, tumor cells in the macrophage-densest quintile hold about 0.43 of drug exposure. In the sparsest, about 0.86 — a 2.0x spread inside a single tumor, where a well-mixed compartmental model returns exactly 1.0 by construction. That flatters us. Tissue-edge distance alone, with no immune input, reproduces 82% of it; nulls preserving the macrophages' clustering return 1.66 and 1.85. What these macrophages add is 1.08-1.21x — smaller, and measured. A compartment expresses none of it. No shipped run carries the immune drug-loss term this needs: it did not improve the measured human-glioma anchor.

05 — Stated limits

The limits ship with it.

We test what we ship, and publish what breaks.

Carried by every run · none of this is buried

Tissue capacity
ASSUMED 1.0
Every compound is given a capacity of 1.0; measured rat brain-slice values run 3.2 to 4184 (n = 33), assumed to carry to human tissue. It cannot move a converged field at all — checked to one part in a billion — but the verdict in 03 is read off a finite exposure window, and there it does move.
Close analogues
0.498–0.839, NOT 0.718
0.718 was scored on the paper's donor counts, in a convention the model does not use. Redone on the engine's, 45 of 71 rows pin exactly and 26 have no published structure — until those are obtained, R² sits anywhere in that interval. It contains 0.718: nothing refuted, nothing confirmed, and a band that wide settles nothing. It still recovers only ~24% of the measured spread, from one cell line, under-separating a congeneric series ~4-fold. Its predecessor scored 0.022, below the whole interval; we published that refutation, then its replacement, and now this.
Spatial spread
1.08–1.21×, NOT 2.0×
A well-mixed model's 1.0 is not what bounds the raw 2.0×. Two spatially-aware nulls return 1.66 and 1.85, and tissue-edge distance alone — no immune input — reproduces 82% of it.
Spot platforms
NOT REPORTED
The spread needs measured cell positions. Spot data estimates them, so the number computes but cannot be attributed — which is the entire claim.
Absolute kill fraction
NOT DEFENSIBLE
Not on any grid, not on any tissue. It is not an output, and it is not on this page.
Patient response
OUT OF SCOPE
Not predicted. Not for diagnostic or treatment-planning use. Null results published alongside positive ones.
06 — Get started

Run it on your own tissue.

The solve is a single process on your own machine, and it sends no tissue or compound data anywhere. De-identified tissue only. Bring a section and a compound; get back a per-cell verdict, the map that is its evidence, and the limits below attached to both — your tissue, your numbers.

What the answer above rests on

The 03 verdict
INVERTS THREE WAYS
It comes from a one-way vascular leak with no path from tissue back to blood, which misses human microdialysis 12.8× high on one drug and 10.5× low on another: no constant fixes both. Supply the missing term and the dominant limiter flips for 7 of 12 compounds, 03's included, relabelling 57.2% of its cells. Moving one conventional band threshold to the median survivor's own exposure flips it again. So does another section of the same tumour — the only one of the three that is tissue and not a choice of ours.
“Arrives almost everywhere”
2.2–3.0× TOO SHALLOW
The map's one out-of-sample perivascular gradient is that much too shallow, from a transport length measured 2.45× too long. The error flatters the drug: it under-reports how much tumour goes untreated. So does the specimen — of three glioblastoma sections, 03's is the most penetrable.
“Under-dosed”
NOT “RAISE THE DOSE”
Most of those cells are flagged resistant too, so more drug alone would not have killed them. And no delivery verdict has been reached non-degenerately: the only one came from a compound whose barrier term is zero — an input, not a discovery.
