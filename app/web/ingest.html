Bring your own tissue

Point this at a directory on the machine running this server holding a 10x Xenium or Space Ranger export (or a bare MEX triple beside a coordinates table). The bytes stay where they are and are read in place — a section is registered by its path, so nothing is copied or transferred anywhere. Browse the server's directories below instead of typing a path: each one is checked as it is listed, and the panel on the right says what the sample is and what it cannot support before anything is added. It drives POST /api/sections/scan — the same endpoint and the same report as vivum ingest.
